01 / About

About Me

A practical, curious, and detail-oriented approach to mechanical engineering.

I am a Mechanical and Electrical Engineer specializing in Mechanical Power, with experience in engineering research, design, fabrication support, and industrial maintenance. My work combines technical problem-solving with practical field experience.

Since March 2025, I have worked as an Engineer at Hama Cement Plant, designing components subject to wear, supervising their manufacturing, preparing maintenance reports, and supporting technical interventions during emergency breakdowns.

My professional background also includes engineering research and freelance work through Mostaql and Khamsat, preparing research papers and presentations, and translating technical research from Arabic into English. I hold a British Board certificate in Trainer Preparation.
